# Inayati / عنايتي

Inayati helps patients know what to do after a screening result, when to do it, and who will follow up. The Android competition prototype runs an offline case with fictional clinic records.

- [Download the Android app and quick start](https://github.com/bakkey/inayati-review/releases/tag/v0.5.0-synthetic)
- [Watch the competition demo](https://youtu.be/xNMKw9Ix-X8)
- [Read the technical documentation online](docs/README.md)
- [Download the full technical guide and archive (PDF)](https://github.com/bakkey/inayati-review/releases/download/v0.5.0-synthetic/Inayati-Full-Technical-Documentation.pdf)

Open the app on Android 12 or later and choose **Start my care journey**. Authorised Abu Dhabi EMR and e-prescription integration is the intended next step.
