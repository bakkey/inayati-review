# Try the Inayati Android prototype

1. Open the [reviewer release](https://github.com/bakkey/inayati-review/releases/tag/v0.5.0-synthetic) on Android 12 or later. Download the APK and follow your device’s installation prompts.
2. Open Inayati and select **Start my care journey**. Review Noor’s fictional screening result. **Care**, **Timeline** and **Care team** show the next step and its source.
3. For the timing case, choose **Demo scenario controller**, then **Load canonical late-dose scenario**. Open the patient care space and select **Timeline**. If already in the patient space, reach **Scenario controller** from **Profile and privacy**.
4. Look for the next dose at **21:20** and the check-in already due at **09:50**. Try a corrected lab or a stopped order in the controller, then return to the timeline.
5. Try Arabic from the welcome screen or **Profile and privacy**. Use **Reset everything** in the controller to replay the case.

The app opens without an account and the case runs offline. The public case uses simulated service events. The connected workflow was tested separately on two enrolled Android emulators.
