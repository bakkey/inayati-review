# Future improvements and pilot

| Phase | Planned work | Evidence before expanding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Secure test deployment | Public-facing HTTPS test relay, scoped enrolment, revocation, recovery and monitoring | Independent review and recovery/revocation tests on two physical devices |
| Authorised clinical integration | Start with read-only lab and prescription feeds. Agree source authority, patient matching, timezone, units and corrections with a clinic. | Partner sandbox and approval of the actual interface and failure handling |
| Physical-device reliability | English and Arabic speech, reminders, offline recovery, accessibility, denial and cancellation | Repeatable test matrix across physical devices and settings |
| Safer optional AI | Evaluate local identifier detection and retrieval, preserve source citations, and test unsupported and stale requests | Bilingual grounding, privacy and consent test results |
| Medication support | Licensed, versioned product information, followed by separate review of interactions, contraindications and refills | Clinician and pharmacist review of sources and workflows |

A pilot needs an accountable clinical owner, a stated response process, and agreement on the eligible population and baseline. Measure patient understanding, time to an accepted follow-up, completed follow-up, staff workload and safety incidents.
